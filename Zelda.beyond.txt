# Hand-made rooms below the bottom edge of the map (rooms 74 .. 7B). Everything else beyond the map is made by the program:
# mountains and trees repeat outwards, the sea goes on east, above the two top right screens and below the right-hand screens.
# Where a patch ends, the sea goes on. Save this file and the game picks it up within a second.
#
# A square is 16 x 16 pixels (a room is 16 squares wide and 11 tall). The legend gives a character its four tiles, reading order
# top-left top-right bottom-left bottom-right (or one tile id for all four), and the palette 0-3 of the area (see the palettes of the
# border rooms in edge_dump.txt: rooms 74-78 use palettes 2 and 3). Tile ids are hex. Do not use '#' as a character.
# Built in (you can redefine them):
   legend ~ = 90 90 95 95 pal 3      sea
   legend - = sq 5 pal 2            green sea
   legend . = 26 pal 3               ground
   legend 1 = sq 30 pal 2            green bottom-left shore
   legend 2 = sq 31 pal 2            green bottom-right shore
   legend m = D8 DA D9 DB pal 3      brown mountain wall
   legend M = D8 DA D9 DB pal 2      green mountain wall
   legend t = C4 C6 C5 C7 pal 3      brown trees
   legend T = C4 C6 C5 C7 pal 2      green trees
   legend 6 = sq 06 pal 2            green up shore
   legend 8 = sq 08 pal 2            green left shore
   legend Q = sq 15 pal 2            green top-left shore
# Tile ids seen on the map: water interior 90 (even row) / 95 (odd row), left shore 8E, right shore 92, top edge 8F, top corners 8D / 91,
# bottom edge 94 / 96 / 98; ground 26; mountain wall D8 DA D9 DB; scalloped mountain top CE D0 CF D1; trees C4-C7.
#
# A patch: the room it hangs under, the number of rows (squares, 1-20), then that many lines of exactly 16 characters.
